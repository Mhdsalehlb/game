How it works

1

Calibrate in seconds

Follow a glowing dot with your eyes — center, left, right, up, down. Stargaze verifies every step and learns your exact range of motion before you begin.

2

Weave with your gaze

A luminous orb follows wherever you look. Rest it on a star until the ring fills — the star lights, and golden threads weave the constellation together.

3

Grow, calmly

No lives, no game over, no time pressure. Levels deepen instead of speeding up, and every third one ends in a guided breathing moment.

Each level trains a different attention skill

🌌 Sustained attention

The dwell itself — holding your gaze steady — is the core exercise. Stillness is rewarded, twitchiness is not.

🧭 Guided shifting

Path levels move your attention deliberately from point to point, like a slow breath for your eyes.

🔢 Visual search & sequencing

Ordered levels ask you to find numbered stars and light them in sequence — search, hold, repeat.

🧠 Working memory

Memory levels flash the pattern once, then ask you to reproduce it unaided. Mistakes gently replay it.

🕯️ Distraction resistance

Drifting embers cross the sky. Letting your gaze linger on one breaks your streak — noticing without following is the skill.

🌬️ Breathing interludes

Guided 4-in / 4-out breathing between levels keeps the session a wind-down, not a workout.

Questions

Does it really track my eyes?

Yes — it uses your front camera and MediaPipe Face Mesh (478 facial landmarks, including your irises) running inside your browser. You can also choose head-only control, or a touch/keyboard mode.

What do I need?

A phone, tablet, or laptop with a front camera and a modern browser (Safari on iPhone works great). Even, soft lighting on your face gives the best tracking.

Is my camera footage uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your device, in the browser tab. Nothing is recorded or transmitted.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes — Stargaze is a progressive web app. On iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen.” On Android/desktop Chrome: use the “Install” option in the menu. You get an app icon and it opens full-screen.

Is this a medical treatment?

No. Stargaze is a calm game built around attention-exercise patterns. It makes no medical claims and is not a substitute for professional care.
